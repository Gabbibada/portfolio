About
01

I'm a Creative Technologist operating at the rare intersection of art, technology, and storytelling — not as a generalist, but as someone who has mastered multiple disciplines at senior level simultaneously.

Nigerian-born. England-based. Globally minded. My career has been the answer to a single frustration: brilliant technical systems built without soul, and visually stunning creative work built without infrastructure.

I realised early that the future belongs to people who can bridge both worlds — those who understand aesthetics and engineering, emotion and systems, narrative and execution.

The Nod
Senior Product Designer Odiotech Social Commerce iOS & Android Canada
Nod
Canada's First Black-Owned Social Commerce Platform  ·  User & Admin
App Screens / UI

The Nod App is Canada's first social e-commerce platform built to intentionally recirculate $100M through 500+ Canadian Black-owned businesses — a movement disguised as an app, designed to transform everyday purchases into systemic community wealth.

As Senior Product Designer at Odiotech, I designed both the User App and the Admin platform — two distinct products serving two entirely different audiences, but sharing a single design language and a common mission.

The User App needed to feel empowering and intuitive: Radar (real-time discovery), NodWallet (community finance), Groups (collective buying), Care (anonymous giving), and Spin & Win (engagement rewards). The Admin platform needed power and clarity — tools for business owners to manage listings, track impact, and serve their community with confidence.

Tank

Nord Motion is Africa's premier automobile corporation — a bold, continent-defining statement about what African industry can build and bring to market. The Nord Tank is its most rugged expression: a vehicle built to command attention, inspire confidence, and signal a new era for African motoring.

I directed the very first commercials that introduced the Nord Tank to the market — the launch films that would define the vehicle's personality before the world had ever seen it move. This is the work that exists at the sharpest intersection of my craft — filmmaking, brand strategy, and commercial direction — all three disciplines required simultaneously, under the highest possible creative standard.
